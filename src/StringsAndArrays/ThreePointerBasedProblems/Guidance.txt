When to use Three Pointer
*************************

Use when:

Problem involves three sorted arrays
Find common elements from 3 arrays
Merge 3 sorted lists
Need to maintain three boundaries or partitions
Dutch National Flag (low, mid, high → 3 pointers)
Triplet-based problems
3-sum pattern (fix 1 pointer, move the other 2)

Want linear O(n) or O(n + m + p) time without extra space
Usually O(1) space.
