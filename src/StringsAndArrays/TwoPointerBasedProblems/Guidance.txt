When should use two pointer approach?
*************************************

Arrays/Strings are sorted
Examples:
********

Two-sum (sorted)
Merge two sorted arrays
Remove duplicates
Find pair with given sum
Sliding window problems (left, right pointers)

You must avoid extra space : - Two-pointer is usually O(1) space.

Need linear traversal instead of nested loops : - Saves time from O(n²) → O(n)
